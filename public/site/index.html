Learn a language by reading

ReadFluent turns real books into reading you can finish: retold at your level, one short page at a time.

Open ReadFluent

How it works

  1. Pick your level. From A1 (beginner) to C2 (advanced). Every book is retold for the level you choose.
  2. Read one page at a time. Each page is a picture and one to three sentences, so a few minutes a day is enough.
  3. Tap any word. See what it means in that sentence, hear it said aloud, and save it as a flashcard to practise later.

What is inside

Classic books retold for learners, and new stories written for them: mysteries, romance, adventure, science, self-help and more. Phrase decks, tests and level exams show how far you have come, and points, streaks and a weekly league with friends keep you going.

The whole app is available in 20 languages, and a book can open in the language you are learning.

An account is needed to read. You can sign in with Apple or Google.
